Sample Rate

IEEE 1159 does not mandate a sample rate. IEC 61000-4-30 Class A practice is ≥256 samples/cycle (~15.4 kHz @ 60 Hz). For Pika’s 1-cycle RMS sag/swell logging, ~2–4 kHz is a practical floor; 10 kHz+ is recommended.

Rates below 120 S/s cannot faithfully represent 60 Hz mains (aliasing).
